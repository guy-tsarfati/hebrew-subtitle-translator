# CueIvrit Code of Conduct

## Purpose

CueIvrit welcomes people who want to improve English-to-Hebrew subtitle translation, tools, and documentation. Participation is open to people of all backgrounds, identities, abilities, and experience levels.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, wiki contributions, and other spaces operated by the project. It also applies when someone represents CueIvrit in an official capacity.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work with courtesy and patience. Explain concerns with concrete examples and suggest improvements.
- Make room for beginners, different communication styles, and different approaches to translation.
- Critique a proposed change without judging the person who proposed it.
- Respect people's names, pronouns, privacy, and boundaries.
- Correct mistakes openly, acknowledge useful feedback, and help keep conversations productive.

Hebrew and English contributions are welcome. Translation choices can be disputed. Explain meaning, context, and evidence, and accept that maintainers may choose a different solution.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal insults, sexual harassment, and unwanted sexual content are prohibited. So are sharing another person's private information without permission, repeated unwanted contact, impersonation, spam, and retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

Disagreement with a technical or editorial decision is not itself misconduct. Repeatedly targeting a participant, intimidating them, or disrupting a discussion after a moderation request may violate this policy.

## Reporting a concern

Send a private email to **Guy Tsarfati**, the project maintainer, at **guy@tsarfati.com**, with the subject **CueIvrit conduct report**.

Where available, include links to the relevant conversation, a brief account of what happened, and any immediate concern about further contact. Share only information necessary to understand the incident. You may report behavior you experienced or witnessed.

Do not put sensitive incident details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities have a separate reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Information may be shared with people needed to assess or address the incident. The maintainer will explain any necessary disclosure when practical. No fixed response time is promised.

If a report concerns the maintainer or you are uncomfortable contacting him, you may use GitHub's platform reporting tools for behavior covered by GitHub's policies. This is an external route, not a separate CueIvrit review team.

## Moderation and enforcement

The maintainer considers the behavior, its impact, available evidence, and any prior incidents. Depending on severity, action may include:

1. A request to change behavior or correct a contribution.
2. A warning explaining the problem and expectations.
3. A temporary restriction on participation.
4. Removal from project spaces for serious or repeated misconduct.

Serious incidents may warrant immediate restrictions. Moderation can include hiding or removing content, closing or locking a conversation, or declining a contribution. Where practical, the affected person receives an explanation.

A participant may reply privately with relevant clarification or request reconsideration. The maintainer will consider new evidence. Attempts to evade restrictions or retaliate may lead to further action.
