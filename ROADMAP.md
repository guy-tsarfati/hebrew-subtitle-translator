# CueIvrit roadmap

This roadmap records priorities, not delivery dates. Track launch progress in [issue #1](https://github.com/guy-tsarfati/hebrew-subtitle-translator/issues/1).

## First public release

- Verify clean installation through the skills CLI and document the tested command.
- Rebuild and audit the Sintel example from the installed files.
- Publish versioned release notes and a downloadable v0.1.0 baseline.
- Add feedback guidance, maintain GitHub Actions dependencies, and require CI checks before merging.

## Demonstration and community feedback

- Verify Hebrew subtitle rendering against the film in an available player.
- Add a self-authored example for SDH and external-credit cleanup.
- Publish a short demonstration and installation walkthrough.
- Collect reproducible installation, translation, and player-display reports.
- Check listing/discovery in skill directories and submit to curated lists when their requirements are met.

## Quality improvements guided by evidence

- Expand regression cases for real reported failures.
- Improve gender/context guidance and recurring terminology with reviewed examples.
- Document player-specific RTL behavior where tested.
- Keep source timing and cue identity auditable throughout the workflow.

Ideas are welcome through [Issues](https://github.com/guy-tsarfati/hebrew-subtitle-translator/issues). Changes remain subject to review, available maintainer time, and the workflow contract in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).
